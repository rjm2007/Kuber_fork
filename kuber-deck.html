The problem

Manual B2B outreach doesn't scale for a manufacturer selling to hundreds of plastics processors.

Finding buyers, researching each company by hand, and writing every first email individually caps how many prospects a small sales team can realistically reach — and most of that time isn't spent selling.

✕ Before Kuber

  • Leads sourced ad hoc, no consistent record of who was contacted
  • Company research done manually, one browser tab at a time
  • Every outreach email drafted from scratch
  • No visibility into reply rates or campaign performance

✓ With Kuber

  • Structured lead database sourced from Apollo, deduped by org
  • Company profiles auto-enriched by scraping + LLM extraction
  • Outreach drafts generated for review, not written cold
  • Campaign, reply, and enrichment status tracked end to end
The solution

One pipeline from raw contact data to a reviewed email, with a human approving every send.

Kuber chains four external services behind a single Next.js backend: Apollo for people and company data, Firecrawl to read a company's own website, an LLM to turn that into usable sales context, and a review queue before anything reaches a prospect's inbox.

Architecture

The enrichment & outreach pipeline

Each stage writes its own status back to the lead or organization, so the UI can show exactly where a record is — queued, scraping, done, or failed — instead of a black box.

Engineering decisions

A few choices worth calling out

Self-chaining queue
The scrape route re-triggers itself with a fire-and-forget fetch instead of Next.js's after(), which proved unreliable in dev — every org gets processed regardless of environment.
Bounded retries
Each organization gets up to 3 enrichment attempts before moving to a permanent-failure state, so a bad site never loops forever.
LLM fallback chain
Extraction and drafting try OpenRouter first, fall back to OpenAI directly, and finally degrade to a static response rather than blocking the pipeline.
Domain inference
When Apollo has no company domain on record, Kuber derives one from the majority domain of that org's known lead emails, ignoring free/webmail providers, and tags the source for transparency.
Auditable enrichment
Every enrichment event is written to an append-only, RLS-scoped log — visible in the UI as a timeline, never mutated after the fact.
What it does

End-user features

SOURCE

Lead search & import

Apollo-backed search or Excel upload with column mapping, deduped into the lead database.

ENRICH

Live enrichment status

A status dot per lead (queued / scraping / done / failed) with a pollable timeline and manual retry.

DRAFT

AI-drafted outreach

Generated emails a rep reviews, edits, approves, or rejects before anything sends.

RUN

Campaign management

Groups leads and drafts into campaigns and tracks each lead's status through the sequence.

REPLY

Unibox & Outbox

Inbound replies threaded against the original send, with reply-from-any-message support.

MEASURE

Analytics dashboard

Sent vs. no-reply-yet breakdown and reply-rate tracking per campaign.
